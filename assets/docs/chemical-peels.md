Chemical peels are advanced resurfacing treatments designed to
improve skin tone, texture, pigmentation, acne, and signs of ageing.
The Perfect Peel and Superblend Peel are glutathione-powered peels
designed to support skin brightening, rejuvenation, and overall skin
health. The peel solution exfoliates and accelerates skin cell
turnover, encouraging healthier new skin to develop. The Perfect Peel
range contains glutathione, a powerful antioxidant known for its
brightening and skin rejuvenating properties, and allows more
localised treatment directly into the skin, helping to target
pigmentation, uneven skin tone, and dullness within the treatment
area. The treatment helps improve pigmentation, acne and congestion,
acne scarring, uneven skin texture, fine lines, and dull or
tired-looking skin.

This treatment is suitable for pigmentation concerns, acne-prone
skin, acne scarring, fine lines and ageing concerns, uneven texture,
dull skin, and general skin rejuvenation. Chemical peels may not be
suitable for anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding, has active skin
infections or cold sores, has open wounds or broken skin, has
recently used Roaccutane, has severe eczema, psoriasis, or dermatitis
in the treatment area, has recently had excessive sun exposure or
sunburn, or has certain allergies to peel ingredients.

Before treatment, clients should avoid retinol and exfoliating
products, avoid waxing and excessive sun exposure, follow any
prescribed skin preparation protocol, and inform their practitioner
of any medications, allergies, or medical conditions. The peel
solution is applied to cleansed skin and left for a specific time
depending on the treatment, and some tingling, warmth, or tightness
may be experienced during application.

Peeling and flaking may occur over several days following treatment,
with the skin continuing to regenerate and brighten over the
following weeks. Clients are advised to keep the skin hydrated, avoid
picking peeling skin, avoid excessive heat and exercise temporarily,
wear SPF daily, and follow all aftercare advice carefully. Downtime
varies depending on the treatment strength and individual skin
response.

Possible temporary side effects include redness, tightness, dryness,
peeling, and sensitivity. Although uncommon, irritation, prolonged
redness, cold sore flare-ups, or post-inflammatory pigmentation may
occur if aftercare advice is not followed correctly.

Chemical peels are often combined with medical skincare,
microneedling, polynucleotides, exosome therapy, hydrafacial, and LED
therapy.
