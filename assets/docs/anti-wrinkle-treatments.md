Anti-wrinkle treatments are injectable treatments designed to
temporarily relax targeted facial muscles responsible for dynamic
lines and wrinkles. These treatments help soften expression lines
whilst maintaining natural facial movement and expression by
temporarily reducing muscle activity in specific areas of the face.
Commonly treated areas include forehead lines, frown lines, crow's
feet, bunny lines, chin dimpling, jawline slimming, and excessive
sweating.

This treatment may be suitable for clients concerned with fine lines
and wrinkles, preventative ageing, excessive muscle movement, teeth
grinding or jaw tension, and excessive sweating. A consultation is
always carried out to determine suitability, and treatment may not be
suitable for anyone who is pregnant, breastfeeding, has certain
neurological conditions, active infections, or allergies to
ingredients.

Before treatment, clients should avoid alcohol for 24 hours
beforehand, avoid blood-thinning medication where medically
appropriate, arrive with clean skin where possible, and inform their
practitioner of any medical conditions, medications, or previous
reactions. Following consultation and assessment, small amounts of
product are carefully injected into the targeted muscles using a fine
needle, with treatment generally quick and well tolerated, typically
taking 15-30 minutes.

Results are not immediate and typically begin developing over several
days, with full results visible after approximately 2 weeks. Clients
are advised to avoid touching or massaging the area, avoid exercise
for 24 hours, avoid excessive heat and alcohol temporarily, and remain
upright for several hours following treatment. A 2-week review
appointment may be recommended. Results typically last around 3-4
months depending on the individual, and most clients tolerate
treatment very well with only mild discomfort.

Temporary side effects may include redness, mild swelling, bruising,
tenderness, and temporary headache. Although uncommon, asymmetry,
temporary heaviness, or eyelid drooping can occur.

Anti-wrinkle treatments are commonly combined with skin boosters,
microneedling, hydrafacials, and medical skincare.

In the UK, botulinum toxin products used for anti-wrinkle treatment
are prescription-only medicines. Licensed indications vary by
product, and treatment must follow an appropriate prescription and
product-specific information. Rare but serious systemic effects,
including iatrogenic botulism, have been reported.
