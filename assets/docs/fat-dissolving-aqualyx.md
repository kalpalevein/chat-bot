Aqualyx is a fat dissolving injectable treatment designed to target
small areas of stubborn fat. The solution breaks down fat cells,
which are then naturally removed by the body over time. This
treatment is suitable for clients concerned with localised stubborn
fat areas, though it may not be suitable during pregnancy,
breastfeeding, active infection, or certain medical conditions.

Before treatment, clients should stay hydrated, avoid alcohol, and
inform their practitioner of medical conditions. The product is
injected into the treatment area using a fine needle or cannula.
Swelling is expected after treatment and is very common, often
lasting several days.

Possible temporary side effects include swelling, bruising,
tenderness, redness, and temporary firmness. A course of treatments
is often recommended depending on the treatment area and concern.

Aqualyx is often combined with weight management support, HIFU, and
body contouring.
