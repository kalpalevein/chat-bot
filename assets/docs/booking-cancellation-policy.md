Booking, Cancellation & Clinic Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026

By booking an appointment with Skin & Aesthetics by Haleema Saadia, you confirm that you have read, understood and agree to these policies.

Appointment Lateness

Please ensure you attend all appointments on time.

If you arrive more than 5 minutes late, your appointment may not be able to go ahead and you may need to rebook. Charges may apply.

Where possible, your appointment may still proceed, but your treatment time may be reduced.

Consent Form Policy

Please complete all required consent forms before your appointment.

Failure to complete consent forms prior to your appointment may reduce your treatment time or may result in your appointment needing to be rescheduled.

Booking Fee Policy

A non-refundable deposit is required to secure all bookings.

The deposit will be deducted from the cost of your treatment.

If you have pre-paid for your treatment or selected a pre-paid finance option and later become entitled to a refund, any applicable service charges, finance fees or administrative charges may be deducted from the refunded amount.

Free Cancellation or Rescheduling

You may cancel or reschedule your appointment without any charge if you do so at least 72 hours before the scheduled appointment time.

Late Cancellation or Rescheduling

For consultations, patch tests and other chargeable treatments or appointments, the full appointment fee will be payable if the appointment is cancelled or rescheduled within 72 hours of the scheduled appointment time.

If you have purchased a treatment package, the missed appointment will be deducted from your treatment package and will count as one completed session of your course.

If you wish to rebook the forfeited session, the full cost of that treatment session will apply.

No-Show Policy

If you do not attend your appointment and do not provide prior notice, the full cost of the scheduled treatment session will be payable.

If you have purchased a treatment package, the missed appointment will be deducted from your treatment package and will count as one completed session of your course.

If you wish to rebook the forfeited session, the full cost of that treatment session will apply.

Why This Policy Exists

This policy allows the clinic to manage its schedule fairly and continue providing the best possible service to all clients.

Treatment Package Policy

All treatment packages are non-refundable once purchased.

However, the remaining value of a package may be transferred to another treatment within the clinic at the discretion of management.

Treatment packages must be completed within 18 months of purchase unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Any unused sessions remaining after this period may be forfeited.

Membership Policy

Memberships are subject to the minimum commitment period stated within the individual membership plan.

Membership payments are non-refundable and will continue for the duration of the minimum term.

Membership benefits, discounts, complimentary treatments, rewards and promotional benefits are available only while the membership remains active and all payments are up to date.

Unused membership benefits and perks do not roll over into future months and cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, refunds or alternative services.

All membership appointments remain subject to the clinic's appointment lateness, cancellation, rescheduling and no-show policies.

If a membership appointment is cancelled, rescheduled or missed within 72 hours of the scheduled appointment time, the appointment may be treated as used and deducted from the member's entitlement.

If the member wishes to rebook the forfeited treatment session, the full cost of that treatment session will apply.

Membership payments contribute towards access to treatments, discounts, exclusive benefits and reserved clinic capacity.

Membership fees remain payable regardless of whether appointments are booked or attended during a particular month.

Memberships may only be cancelled after completion of the applicable minimum membership term.

After the minimum term has been completed, members must provide at least four weeks' written notice before their next billing date to cancel their membership.

Laser Hair Removal During Menstruation

Laser hair removal can be carried out during your menstrual cycle.

However, some clients may feel more sensitive or uncomfortable receiving treatment in the bikini or buttocks areas during this time.

If preferred, treatment can continue on all other scheduled areas and the bikini or buttocks areas can be rescheduled for a later date after your cycle has ended.

This courtesy may be used once per treatment session and applies only to the bikini and buttocks areas.

If you choose to cancel or reschedule your entire appointment within 72 hours of the scheduled appointment time, the standard cancellation policy will apply.

Children and Babies

Children and babies are not permitted to attend appointments.

This policy is in place to maintain a safe clinical environment for clients and staff.

If you arrive with a child or baby, the clinic may be unable to proceed with your treatment and your appointment may need to be cancelled.

In these circumstances, the standard cancellation policy may apply.

Clinical Suitability

All treatments are subject to practitioner assessment, medical suitability, contraindications and clinical judgement at each appointment.

Skin & Aesthetics by Haleema Saadia reserves the right to postpone or decline treatment where it is considered clinically inappropriate or unsafe to proceed.

Treatment Results

Treatment outcomes vary from person to person.

Individual treatment results cannot be guaranteed.

Our Commitment

Skin & Aesthetics by Haleema Saadia is committed to providing clients with a high standard of care, safety and clinical practice.

These policies are in place to provide a fair, consistent and professional experience for clients while respecting appointment times and maintaining appropriate clinical standards.

The clinic appreciates your understanding, cooperation and continued trust.
