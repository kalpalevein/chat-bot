We offer treatments across four main categories: Skin Treatments,
Injectables Treatments, Laser Hair Removal Treatments, and Wellness
Treatments. Every treatment plan is personalised based on your skin type,
concerns, medical history, and goals.

We are experienced in treating a wide range of skin tones and concerns,
including pigmentation, acne scarring, post-inflammatory
hyperpigmentation, and melanin-rich skin.

Your treatment journey follows five stages: Consultation and Assessment,
Personalised Plan, Treatment, Aftercare, and Review and Maintenance.

To support healthier skin between treatments, we also offer Obagi
Medical products, Glutaceuticals, pigmentation management, and acne
support, aimed at maintaining treatment results, improving skin health
over time, and supporting collagen production.
