Prescription weight loss medications are treatments designed to
support weight management alongside healthy lifestyle changes,
nutrition, and exercise. Options include Mounjaro (tirzepatide), a
dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist used for weight management in
eligible adults under prescribing criteria; Wegovy (semaglutide), a
GLP-1 receptor agonist brand used for obesity and weight management;
Ozempic (semaglutide), a semaglutide brand licensed in the UK for
type 2 diabetes rather than as a standard obesity treatment; and
Orlistat, an oral medicine that reduces absorption of dietary fat.
Suitability for any prescription treatment must be assessed following
an appropriate medical consultation.

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist, and tirzepatide acts at both
GIP and GLP-1 receptors. These incretin-based medicines can reduce
appetite, increase feelings of fullness, slow gastric emptying, and
affect glucose regulation, and their use for weight management must
follow the licensed indication and individual prescribing assessment.
Orlistat works differently, inhibiting gastrointestinal lipases and
reducing the amount of dietary fat absorbed from the digestive tract.
Treatment plans are tailored individually depending on medical
history, goals, and suitability.

These medications are suitable for adults who meet prescribing
criteria following medical assessment, including those struggling
with weight management, increased appetite or cravings, or
weight-related health concerns, though they may not be suitable for
everyone. They may not be suitable for anyone who is pregnant or
breastfeeding, has certain gastrointestinal conditions, has a history
of pancreatitis, has certain thyroid conditions, has uncontrolled
medical conditions, or is taking medications that may interact with
treatment. A full consultation and medical assessment is always
carried out.

Before treatment, clients attend a medical consultation, provide full
medical history and medication information, and discuss lifestyle,
goals, and expectations honestly. Following medical assessment and
prescribing approval, treatment plans are tailored individually.
Injectable medications are usually self-administered weekly following
appropriate guidance and support, with regular reviews and monitoring
appointments also recommended. Ongoing support, monitoring, and
lifestyle guidance may be provided throughout treatment, and weight
loss is usually gradual with results varying between individuals.

Possible side effects may include nausea, digestive upset, reduced
appetite, fatigue, headaches, constipation or diarrhoea, and
bloating. Although uncommon, more serious side effects can occur, and
all risks, suitability, and safety information are discussed during
consultation. Weight loss varies between individuals and depends on
lifestyle, consistency, and treatment suitability, and healthy
nutrition, hydration, exercise, and lifestyle habits remain important
throughout treatment.

Weight loss medications are often combined with wellness support,
vitamin injections, and body contouring treatments.
