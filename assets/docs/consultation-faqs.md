Consultation frequently asked questions:

How long does a consultation take? Initial consultations typically last
approximately 30 minutes. Longer appointments may be arranged for
clients with multiple concerns or more detailed treatment planning needs.

Do I need treatment on the same day? Treatment can often be carried out
on the same day where suitable and where appointment availability
allows. Alternatively, treatment can be booked for a later date.

Is the consultation fee redeemable? Yes, consultation fees are fully
redeemable against eligible treatments or medical-grade skincare
purchases.

Do I need to bring anything to my consultation? Just yourself. However,
if relevant, please bring details of medications, medical history,
previous treatments, or skincare products you are currently using.

Can I discuss multiple concerns in one consultation? Yes, you are welcome
to discuss more than one concern during your appointment. If you need a
more detailed consultation, let us know when booking so enough time can
be allocated.

Do I need a patch test for laser treatments? Yes, a patch test is
required before laser hair removal to assess your skin response and
treatment suitability.
