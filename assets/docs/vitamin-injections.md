Vitamin B, C and D injections are intramuscular injections designed to
support general wellbeing, energy levels, immunity, and overall
health. The vitamins are delivered directly into the muscle, allowing
for efficient absorption into the bloodstream.

This treatment is suitable for clients concerned with fatigue, low
energy, immune support, vitamin deficiencies, and general wellness
support. Suitability is always assessed during consultation.

Before treatment, clients should eat and hydrate normally and inform
their practitioner of any medical conditions or medications. The
injection is administered into the muscle, commonly the upper arm,
and treatment typically takes 10-15 minutes. Clients can usually
return to normal activities immediately afterwards.

Possible side effects include mild tenderness, bruising, redness, and
temporary soreness at the injection site. Some clients notice
benefits quickly, whilst others may notice more gradual improvements.
How often treatments can be repeated depends on the vitamin type,
individual needs, and practitioner recommendation. Suitability for
treatment depends on medical history, allergies, medications, and
overall health, and will always be assessed during consultation.

Vitamin injections are often combined with wellness treatments,
weight management support, and hydration and skin health treatments.
