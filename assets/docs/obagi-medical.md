Obagi Medical is a professional skincare brand used within aesthetic
and skin-health settings. Its product ranges are designed to support
concerns such as uneven skin tone, pigmentation, acne-prone skin,
dehydration, fine lines, wrinkles, texture changes, and visible signs
of ageing. At Skin & Aesthetics by Haleema, Obagi Medical products
are referenced as part of professional facial treatments and tailored
medical-grade homecare plans.

Obagi Medical is used in Obagi Facials and as part of medical-grade
skincare support. These facials are professional treatments designed
to deeply cleanse the skin, exfoliate the skin, improve hydration,
support brighter-looking skin, improve overall skin quality, and
target specific skin concerns using tailored products, with the
products selected according to the client's skin concerns and
treatment goals.

Several Obagi facial options are offered by the clinic. The Signature
Facial is a medical-grade facial focused on cleansing, hydration,
brightness, and skin refreshment, suitable for clients looking for
smoother-looking skin, improved hydration, a fresher complexion, and
general skin maintenance. The Anti-Oxidant Facial focuses on
antioxidant support using Vitamin C-rich Obagi products, aiming to
brighten dull-looking skin, support a more even-looking complexion,
support collagen-related skin health, and help protect the skin from
environmental oxidative stress. The Acne Express Facial is intended
for congested and acne-prone skin, helping to deeply cleanse pores,
reduce excess surface oil, calm the appearance of inflammation, and
support clearer-looking skin. The Age Defying Facial is a
rejuvenating facial focused on fine lines, skin texture, hydration,
visible signs of ageing, and skin radiance, and may include active
anti-ageing and antioxidant ingredients selected for the client's
needs. The Enrich Facial is a hydrating and nourishing facial
intended to restore moisture, support the skin barrier, improve
overall skin comfort, and support a healthier-looking glow.

Obagi facials may be considered for dull skin, congested skin,
acne-prone skin, mild pigmentation, dehydrated skin, fine lines,
ageing concerns, and general skin maintenance. The wider Obagi range
is also designed around concerns such as uneven skin tone, dark spots
and discolouration, uneven texture, fine lines and wrinkles,
dehydration, acne and blemishes, sensitive skin, and visible signs of
ageing.

Obagi's official product range includes categories such as cleansers,
toners, Vitamin C products, retinoid and retinol products, serums and
targeted treatments, moisturisers, sunscreens, acne-focused skincare,
pigmentation-focused skincare, and hydration and barrier-support
products. Not every Obagi product is suitable for every client.

Vitamin C is used in Obagi skincare for antioxidant support and to
help improve the appearance of dullness and uneven skin tone.
Retinoid-based skincare can support skin renewal and help improve the
appearance of fine lines, photoageing, and uneven texture, though
these products can cause dryness, irritation, or increased
sensitivity in some users and should be introduced appropriately.
Some Obagi products use hydrating ingredients such as hyaluronic acid
or related humectants to support skin hydration. Broad-spectrum
sunscreen is an important part of pigmentation management,
anti-ageing skincare, and post-treatment skin protection.

Obagi skincare is not a single universal routine. A suitable plan
depends on skin type, skin sensitivity, current skin condition,
pigmentation pattern, acne activity, existing skincare products,
current aesthetic treatments, medical history, pregnancy or
breastfeeding status where relevant, and use of prescription or
active skincare. A practitioner may recommend a simple maintenance
routine or a more structured programme depending on the client's
needs.

The clinic references medical skincare alongside several treatments,
including chemical peels, microneedling, RF microneedling,
hydrafacial, polynucleotides, and injectable skin treatments. The
exact timing of active skincare before and after a procedure should
be decided by the treating practitioner, and retinoids and
exfoliating acids may need to be paused around certain procedures.

Depending on the product and active ingredients, temporary effects
may include dryness, tightness, redness, flaking, tingling, and
increased skin sensitivity. Customers should stop or reduce a product
and seek professional advice if they develop significant irritation
or an unexpected reaction.

Professional guidance is especially important for people who have
sensitive or reactive skin, have active eczema, dermatitis, or another
inflammatory skin condition, are receiving prescription skin
treatment, use strong retinoids or exfoliating acids, are preparing
for a chemical peel, microneedling, RF treatment, or laser procedure,
are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of significant
product reactions.

Obagi Medical is best viewed as a professional skincare system that
can be tailored to individual skin concerns, rather than a single
product or one-size-fits-all routine. At Skin & Aesthetics by
Haleema, the clinic uses Obagi products within professional facials
and may also recommend medical-grade homecare to support longer-term
skin health and maintain treatment results.

Product availability, formulations, prescription status, and
recommended use can vary by country and may change over time.
Customers should follow current product labelling and practitioner
advice.
