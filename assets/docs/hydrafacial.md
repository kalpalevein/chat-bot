Hydrafacial is a non-invasive facial treatment designed to deeply
cleanse, exfoliate, extract impurities, hydrate, and rejuvenate the
skin. The treatment can be customised using different technologies
and medical-grade skincare depending on individual skin concerns.

Several types of Hydrafacial are offered. The Anti-Ageing Hydrafacial
focuses on hydration, fine lines, collagen support, and overall skin
rejuvenation using targeted anti-ageing ingredients. The Detox
Hydrafacial is designed to deeply cleanse congested skin, improve
circulation, support lymphatic drainage, and refresh dull or
tired-looking skin. The Acne Hydrafacial targets congestion, excess
oil, breakouts, and inflammation using medical-grade Obagi skincare
and clarifying ingredients. The Gluta-Brightening Hydrafacial is a
brightening-focused facial using the Glutaceuticals skincare range,
designed to improve dullness, uneven skin tone, and pigmentation
concerns whilst supporting overall skin radiance.

Depending on the skin concern, treatment may include a combination of
advanced technologies and tools. The Hydra Handle is used to deeply
cleanse, exfoliate, extract impurities, and infuse hydrating serums
into the skin. High Frequency helps reduce acne-causing bacteria, calm
inflammation, and support clearer skin. A Skin Scrubber uses
ultrasonic vibrations to help remove dead skin cells, excess oil, and
congestion whilst improving product penetration. Microcurrent is a
low-level electrical current used to stimulate facial muscles,
improve skin tone, and provide a lifting effect. PDT LED Light Therapy
supports skin healing, reduces inflammation, calms acne-prone skin,
and stimulates collagen production. Serum Spray Infusion helps
deliver hydrating and targeted serums evenly across the skin for
enhanced absorption and glow. Lymphatic Drainage is a gentle technique
designed to support circulation, reduce puffiness, and encourage
fluid movement within the skin. These technologies are selected
depending on the individual's skin concerns and treatment goals.

Using vortex technology and advanced skincare tools, the treatment
deeply cleanses the skin whilst infusing hydrating and targeted
serums. This treatment is suitable for congested skin, dull skin,
acne-prone skin, dehydrated skin, enlarged pores, fine lines, uneven
skin tone, and general skin maintenance.

Before treatment, clients should avoid harsh exfoliation, active
irritation, and excessive sun exposure. During treatment, the skin is
cleansed, exfoliated, extracted, hydrated, and treated using selected
technologies tailored to individual concerns. Afterwards, the skin
often appears instantly brighter, smoother, cleaner, and more hydrated
with minimal downtime, and most clients experience little to no
downtime following treatment. Monthly maintenance is commonly
recommended.

Possible temporary side effects include mild redness, tightness, and
temporary sensitivity, and mild purging can occur in acne-prone skin.

Hydrafacial is often combined with medical skincare, chemical peels,
microneedling, and LED therapy.

Claims about "detoxification" should be interpreted cautiously.
Lymphatic techniques may support fluid movement and temporarily
reduce puffiness, but broad "toxin removal" claims should not be
treated as established clinical outcomes.
