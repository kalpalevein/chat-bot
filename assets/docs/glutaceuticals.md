Glutaceuticals is a professional skincare range built around
glutathione together with other active ingredients, botanical
extracts, and acids. The brand is designed to support the appearance
of skin tone, texture, radiance, hydration, and the skin's moisture
barrier. At Skin & Aesthetics by Haleema, the clinic references
Glutaceuticals as part of medical-grade skincare and specifically
mentions it in the Gluta-Brightening Hydrafacial.

Glutathione is an antioxidant naturally present in the body. In
topical skincare, glutathione is used mainly for its antioxidant
properties and to support a brighter-looking complexion, a more
even-looking skin tone, protection against oxidative stress, overall
skin radiance, and support for the appearance of healthier skin.
Glutathione should not be presented as a guaranteed treatment for
pigmentation or as a skin-bleaching agent.

The clinic offers a Gluta-Brightening Hydrafacial using the
Glutaceuticals skincare range, intended to focus on dull-looking
skin, uneven skin tone, pigmentation concerns, skin radiance,
hydration, and general skin rejuvenation. The Hydrafacial itself may
combine cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, hydration, serum
infusion, and other technologies depending on the client's skin
needs.

The clinic's chemical-peel section also discusses glutathione-powered
peels, specifically The Perfect Peel and Superblend Peel. These are
separate treatment products from a normal home skincare routine, but
they share the theme of using glutathione as part of a brightening
and rejuvenating approach. These peels are used for concerns
including pigmentation, acne and congestion, acne scarring, uneven
skin texture, fine lines, and dull or tired-looking skin. Chemical
peels are professional procedures and should not be confused with
everyday Glutaceuticals homecare products.

According to the brand's official information, the range combines
glutathione with multiple botanical extracts, acids, and other active
ingredients. The overall goals include helping to refine the
appearance of skin texture, support a more even-looking tone, protect
the skin from oxidative stress, support the skin's moisture barrier,
improve visible radiance, and simplify a targeted skincare routine.

The official Glutaceuticals collection includes products such as
5-a-Day, Hit Reset, As a Lake, Pep Talk, Night Shift, and Front Cover.
The exact product recommended should depend on the client's skin
type, concerns, tolerance, and existing routine, and product names
alone should not be used to determine suitability.

Depending on the specific product and formulation, the range may be
considered as part of a skincare plan for concerns such as
dull-looking skin, uneven-looking tone, pigmentation-prone skin,
dryness or dehydration, skin texture, reduced radiance, environmental
oxidative stress, and general skin maintenance. A consultation is
useful where pigmentation is significant, persistent, or associated
with an underlying skin condition.

The brand positions glutathione as its main antioxidant ingredient
and combines it with other actives rather than relying on glutathione
alone, meaning the effect and tolerability of a particular product
depends on its full ingredient list, not simply the presence of
glutathione. Some products in the range may contain acids or other
active ingredients that require gradual introduction or professional
guidance.

Glutaceuticals is a topical skincare range and should not be confused
with glutathione injections, intravenous glutathione, or oral
glutathione supplements. Topical skincare acts at the skin surface
and within superficial skin layers according to the formulation, and
the clinic's use of Glutaceuticals relates to skincare and facial
treatment, not systemic glutathione administration.

Depending on the specific product, Glutaceuticals may form part of a
routine used around treatments such as hydrafacial, chemical peels,
microneedling, RF microneedling, and other skin-rejuvenation
treatments. However, active ingredients may need to be paused before
or after certain procedures, and the practitioner should confirm when
a product can be safely stopped and restarted.

Depending on the formulation, temporary reactions may include
tingling, dryness, redness, tightness, flaking, and increased
sensitivity. Products containing exfoliating acids or stronger active
ingredients may be more likely to cause irritation if overused or
combined with multiple other actives.

Professional guidance is recommended if the customer has sensitive or
reactive skin, has active eczema, dermatitis, or significant
inflammation, has recently had a chemical peel, microneedling, RF
treatment, or laser procedure, uses prescription retinoids or other
prescription skin treatments, is already using several acids or
exfoliating products, is pregnant or breastfeeding, or has persistent
or worsening pigmentation.

Glutaceuticals is best understood as a glutathione-focused
professional skincare range used to support brighter-looking, more
even, well-hydrated skin while maintaining the skin barrier. At Skin
& Aesthetics by Haleema, it is particularly relevant to
brightening-focused skincare and the clinic's Gluta-Brightening
Hydrafacial.

Specific claims, ingredient lists, and formulations can change.
Customers should use the current product label and follow the
clinic's recommendations for the exact product supplied.
