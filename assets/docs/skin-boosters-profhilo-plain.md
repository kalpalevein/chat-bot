Skin boosters are injectable treatments designed to deeply hydrate
the skin and improve overall skin quality, elasticity, and glow. They
use hyaluronic acid to improve hydration and stimulate collagen
production within the skin. Profhilo specifically spreads beneath the
skin to improve overall skin texture and firmness.

This treatment is suitable for clients concerned with dehydrated
skin, dull skin, fine lines, skin laxity, and loss of skin quality.
Treatment may not be suitable during pregnancy, breastfeeding, active
skin infections, or certain medical conditions.

Before treatment, clients should avoid alcohol where possible, avoid
blood-thinning medications where appropriate, and inform their
practitioner of any medical conditions. Small injections are placed
into targeted areas of the skin, with treatment typically taking
20-30 minutes.

Small bumps may be visible temporarily before the product disperses,
and the skin continues improving over several weeks following
treatment. Results develop gradually, and an initial course of 2-3
treatments is commonly recommended.

Possible temporary side effects include swelling, bruising,
tenderness, redness, and temporary injection site bumps.

Skin boosters are often combined with anti-wrinkle treatments,
polynucleotides, hydrafacial, and medical skincare.
