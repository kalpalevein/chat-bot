Skin Boosters & Profhilo:

What are skin boosters? Skin boosters are injectable treatments
designed to deeply hydrate the skin and improve overall skin quality,
elasticity, and glow.

How do skin boosters work? Skin boosters use hyaluronic acid to
improve hydration and stimulate collagen production within the skin.
Profhilo specifically spreads beneath the skin to improve overall skin
texture and firmness.

Who are skin boosters suitable for? Suitable for clients concerned
with dehydrated skin, dull skin, fine lines, skin laxity, and loss of
skin quality.

How do I prepare for treatment? Avoid alcohol where possible, avoid
blood-thinning medications where appropriate, and inform your
practitioner of any medical conditions.

What happens during treatment? Small injections are placed into
targeted areas of the skin. Treatment typically takes 20-30 minutes.

What happens after treatment? Small bumps may be visible temporarily
before the product disperses. The skin continues improving over
several weeks following treatment.

What are the side effects? Possible temporary side effects include
swelling, bruising, tenderness, redness, and temporary injection site
bumps.

When will I see results? Skin quality improvements continue
developing over several weeks following treatment.

How many sessions are recommended? An initial course of 2-3
treatments is commonly recommended.

Who may not be suitable for treatment? Treatment may not be suitable
during pregnancy, breastfeeding, active skin infections, or certain
medical conditions.

What treatments are often combined with skin boosters? Skin boosters
are often combined with anti-wrinkle treatments, polynucleotides,
hydrafacial, and medical skincare.
