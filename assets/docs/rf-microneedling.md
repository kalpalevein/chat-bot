RF microneedling combines traditional microneedling with
radiofrequency energy to improve skin texture, firmness, and collagen
production. Fine needles create micro-channels whilst radiofrequency
heat is delivered into deeper skin layers to tighten and stimulate
collagen. Advanced serums may also be infused during treatment to
further support skin repair, hydration, regeneration, and
rejuvenation.

Several professional serums are used depending on the individual's
skin concerns and treatment goals. Derma Group Pro SKN Eye is a
targeted eye rejuvenation serum designed to improve hydration, dark
circles, puffiness, and fine lines around the delicate eye area.
Derma Group SKN Face 360 is a comprehensive skin rejuvenation serum
focused on hydration, collagen support, skin glow, elasticity, and
overall skin quality. Derma Group SKN Repair is a restorative serum
designed to support skin recovery, repair, barrier function, and
healing. V-Tech Regenerative Serums are advanced regenerative
formulations used to support collagen stimulation, skin repair,
hydration, and rejuvenation following treatment.

This treatment is suitable for acne scarring, skin laxity, fine
lines, enlarged pores, uneven texture, and ageing skin, and is
commonly used to improve acne scarring and skin texture. It may not
be suitable for anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding, has active
skin infections or cold sores, has open wounds or broken skin, has
certain implanted medical devices such as pacemakers, has poor wound
healing, or has a history of keloid scarring.

Before treatment, clients should avoid active skincare products,
avoid sun exposure, and inform their practitioner of any implanted
medical devices. A numbing cream may be applied before treatment, and
the RF microneedling device is then passed across the skin, with
targeted serums also applied depending on the skin concern being
treated.

The skin may appear red, swollen, or warm temporarily before
gradually improving. Results continue improving over several weeks to
months as collagen stimulation develops, and some redness and
sensitivity are expected temporarily.

Possible temporary side effects include redness, swelling, pinpoint
marks, dryness, and sensitivity.

RF microneedling is often combined with polynucleotides, exosome
therapy, medical skincare, and hydrafacial.

Any claims about exosome-containing or regenerative serums should be
treated as product-specific and evidence-dependent. Marketing claims
should not be generalised to all exosome or regenerative products,
and the regulatory status and intended use of the exact product
should be confirmed before clinical deployment.
