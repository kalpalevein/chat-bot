Skin and Aesthetics by Haleema was founded with a clear philosophy: to
deliver advanced aesthetic treatments in a way that prioritises safety,
skin health, and natural results. Every client journey begins with a
thorough consultation to understand concerns, goals, and skin in detail.

Haleema, Founder and Clinical Lead (MPharm, IPresc, MSc ACP), is a
prescribing pharmacist and advanced aesthetics practitioner with over 10
years of healthcare experience. She qualified as a pharmacist, became an
Independent Prescriber, and completed a Master's in Advanced Clinical
Practice. She believes aesthetic medicine should enhance, not change,
natural features, and delivers every treatment with honesty, precision,
and a patient-first approach.

Natania is the clinic's Senior Laser Hair Removal Practitioner, with over

strong clinical skills, attention to detail, and a calm, reassuring
