Obagi facials are medical-grade skincare treatments designed to
deeply cleanse, exfoliate, hydrate, and improve overall skin health
using professional Obagi Medical products. These facials are tailored
to individual skin concerns and combine advanced skincare with a
results-driven approach.

Several types of Obagi facials are offered. The Signature Facial is a
luxury medical-grade facial designed to deeply cleanse, hydrate,
brighten, and refresh the skin, ideal for clients wanting glowing,
smoother, healthier-looking skin with minimal downtime. The
Anti-Oxidant Facial is a radiance-boosting facial focused on
antioxidant protection using Vitamin C-rich Obagi products to help
brighten dull skin, support collagen production, and defend against
environmental damage. The Acne Express Facial is designed for
congested and acne-prone skin using targeted Obagi Medical products
to deeply cleanse pores, reduce excess oil, calm inflammation, and
support clearer-looking skin. The Age Defying Facial is a
rejuvenating facial focused on fine lines, skin texture, hydration,
and visible signs of ageing, combining active anti-ageing ingredients
and antioxidant support. The Enrich Facial is a deeply nourishing and
hydrating facial designed to restore moisture, strengthen the skin
barrier, and improve overall skin health and glow.

The treatment uses professional-grade Obagi Medical skincare products
to deeply cleanse, exfoliate, hydrate, and target specific skin
concerns. It is suitable for dull skin, congested skin, acne-prone
skin, mild pigmentation, dehydrated skin, fine lines and ageing
concerns, and general skin maintenance, and suitability for sensitive
skin is assessed during consultation.

Before treatment, clients should avoid active irritation and inform
their practitioner of any sensitivities, allergies, or current
skincare use. The skin is cleansed, exfoliated, and treated using
tailored Obagi Medical products selected according to individual
concerns. Afterwards, the skin may appear brighter, smoother, clearer,
and more hydrated immediately.

Possible temporary side effects include mild redness, dryness,
tightness, and temporary sensitivity. Many clients benefit from
regular monthly maintenance treatments, and treatments can be tailored
to support acne and pigmentation concerns.

Obagi facials are often combined with medical skincare, chemical
peels, microneedling, and hydrafacial.
