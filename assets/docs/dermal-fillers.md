Dermal fillers are injectable gel-based treatments used to restore
volume, enhance facial features, and improve facial balance and
contour. Most dermal fillers contain hyaluronic acid, a substance
naturally found within the skin, and the filler adds structure,
volume, hydration, and support to targeted areas. Common treatment
areas include lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, nasolabial folds, and
marionette lines.

This treatment may be suitable for clients wanting to restore lost
volume, enhance facial contours, improve facial symmetry, hydrate and
define lips, or rejuvenate ageing skin. Treatment may not be suitable
during pregnancy or breastfeeding, active skin infections, or certain
medical conditions.

Before treatment, clients should avoid alcohol and blood-thinning
medications where appropriate, avoid dental work close to treatment
dates, and inform their practitioner of any medical conditions or
previous filler treatments. Following consultation and facial
assessment, filler is carefully injected into the chosen areas using
either a needle or cannula, with treatment time typically ranging from
30-60 minutes.

Mild swelling and bruising are common initially, and final results
are usually visible once swelling settles, with some results visible
immediately. Clients are advised to avoid excessive pressure on the
area, avoid exercise and heat for 24-48 hours, stay hydrated, and
follow all aftercare advice carefully. Most dermal fillers contain
lidocaine and topical numbing may also be used to maximise comfort.
Longevity varies depending on the treatment area and product used.

Common side effects may include swelling, bruising, tenderness,
redness, and temporary asymmetry. Although rare, more serious
complications can occur, including infection or vascular occlusion.

Dermal fillers are often combined with anti-wrinkle treatments, skin
boosters, polynucleotides, and medical skincare.
