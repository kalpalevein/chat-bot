Polynucleotides are regenerative injectable treatments designed to
improve skin repair, hydration, elasticity, and overall skin quality.
They work by stimulating fibroblast activity, improving tissue
repair, hydration, and collagen production, and are often used around
delicate areas such as the eyes, face, neck, and hands.

This treatment is suitable for clients concerned with skin ageing,
fine lines, dark circles, crepey skin, dull or tired skin, and skin
repair and regeneration. It may not be suitable during pregnancy,
breastfeeding, active infection, or certain medical conditions.

Before treatment, clients should avoid alcohol where possible, inform
their practitioner of medical conditions or allergies, and avoid
active skin irritation. Small injections are carefully placed into
targeted areas, with treatment generally taking 30-45 minutes.

Mild swelling or small bumps may occur temporarily after treatment,
and results gradually improve over several weeks as collagen
stimulation and tissue repair occur. Common treatment areas include
the under-eyes, face, neck, and hands.

Temporary side effects may include swelling, bruising, tenderness,
redness, and temporary injection marks.

Polynucleotides are often combined with RF microneedling,
microneedling, skin boosters, and medical skincare.
