A laser consultation and patch test are used to assess treatment
suitability before laser hair removal. A patch test is required, and
patch testing helps assess skin response and treatment suitability.
Suitability depends on skin tone, hair colour, medical history,
hormonal influences, hair thickness and density, photosensitive
medical conditions or medications, active skin infection or
irritation, recent fake tan, and recent waxing or plucking of the
treatment area.

Before the appointment, clients should avoid waxing or plucking,
avoid fake tan and excessive sun exposure, inform the practitioner
about medications and medical conditions, and attend the required
patch test. It also helps to bring relevant medication details,
medical information, previous treatment history, and details of
current skincare products where applicable.

The consultation pathway includes consultation and assessment, skin
analysis, medical history review, a personalised treatment plan, and
treatment and aftercare guidance.

The supplied document does not specify a universal waiting period
between patch testing and full treatment, the exact test settings,
test area, or device-specific patch-test protocol. These details are
defined by the clinic's laser safety protocol, manufacturer
instructions, insurer requirements, and applicable professional or
regulatory guidance.
