Microneedling is an advanced skin rejuvenation treatment that uses
fine sterile needles to create controlled micro-channels within the
skin. This stimulates the skin's natural healing response and
encourages collagen and elastin production, helping improve acne
scarring, enlarged pores, fine lines, pigmentation, and uneven
texture. Advanced mesotherapy and regenerative serums may also be
infused during treatment to further enhance hydration, collagen
stimulation, skin repair, and rejuvenation.

Several professional serums are used depending on the individual's
skin concerns and treatment goals. Derma Group Pro SKN Eye is a
targeted serum for the delicate eye area to help improve hydration,
fine lines, dark circles, puffiness, and overall skin quality around
the eyes. Derma Group SKN Face 360 is a multi-functional skin
rejuvenation serum designed to support hydration, collagen
production, brightness, elasticity, and overall skin health. Derma
Group SKN Repair is a restorative serum focused on skin healing,
repair, barrier support, and recovery, particularly beneficial for
compromised, damaged, or sensitised skin. Exosome and polynucleotide
regenerative serums may also be used, where exosomes help support
cellular communication and skin regeneration, whilst polynucleotides
help stimulate fibroblast activity, collagen production, hydration,
and tissue repair. These regenerative serums may help improve skin
healing and recovery, fine lines and wrinkles, skin texture and
elasticity, hydration, dull or tired-looking skin, acne scarring, and
overall skin quality.

This treatment is suitable for acne scarring, fine lines, enlarged
pores, uneven texture, dull skin, and general rejuvenation, and can
often be suitable for a wide range of skin tones when carried out
appropriately. It may not be suitable for anyone who is pregnant or
breastfeeding, has used Roaccutane within the last 6 months, has
active skin infections or cold sores, has open wounds or broken skin,
has poor wound healing, or has a history of keloid or hypertrophic
scarring.

Before treatment, clients should avoid retinol and exfoliating acids,
avoid excessive sun exposure, and inform their practitioner of
medications or skin conditions. A sterile microneedling device is
passed across the skin creating controlled micro-channels, with
targeted serums also applied depending on the skin concern being
treated. Treatment generally takes 30-60 minutes.

The skin may appear red and sensitive temporarily before gradually
improving over the following weeks, as it continues regenerating.

Temporary side effects may include redness, swelling, tightness,
dryness, and mild flaking. A course of 3-6 treatments is often
recommended depending on the skin concern.

Microneedling is often combined with polynucleotides, chemical peels,
hydrafacial, and medical skincare.

Any claims about exosome-containing or regenerative serums should be
treated as product-specific and evidence-dependent. Marketing claims
should not be generalised to all exosome or regenerative products,
and the regulatory status and intended use of the exact product
should be confirmed before clinical deployment.
