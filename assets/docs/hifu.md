HIFU (High Intensity Focused Ultrasound) is a non-surgical skin
tightening and lifting treatment designed to improve skin firmness,
contour, and collagen production. At Skin & Aesthetics by Haleema,
the Ultra 4D Platinum HIFU device by Sculpt Pro is used, an advanced
system designed to target deeper layers of the skin and soft tissue
using focused ultrasound energy. It is designed to stimulate collagen
production, improve skin tightening and lifting, support facial
contouring, improve mild to moderate skin laxity, and support body
contouring and fat reduction. The device uses advanced multi-line
technology capable of delivering multiple treatment lines in one pass
for enhanced precision and efficiency, and includes multiple
treatment depth cartridges allowing treatment to be tailored to the
treatment area and concern.

Focused ultrasound energy is delivered into the deeper layers of the
skin where collagen stimulation and tissue tightening occur. HIFU can
target both skin and subcutaneous fat depending on the treatment area
and cartridge used, and works beneath the skin surface without
damaging the outer skin layer.

This treatment is suitable for clients concerned with mild to
moderate skin laxity, jawline definition, facial contouring, sagging
skin, fine lines and wrinkles, and lower face and neck tightening,
including both facial tightening and selected body contouring
concerns. It may not be suitable for anyone who is pregnant or
breastfeeding, has metal implants in the treatment area, has severe
skin laxity requiring surgical intervention, has active skin
infections or open wounds, or has certain medical conditions affecting
healing.

Before treatment, clients should avoid active skin irritation, inform
their practitioner of medical conditions or implants, and attend
consultation and assessment. A patch test is not required. Ultrasound
energy is delivered through the skin using the Ultra 4D Platinum
handheld applicator, and some warmth, tingling, or mild discomfort may
be experienced during treatment, with treatment time varying
depending on the area treated.

Some clients may notice an initial tightening effect, however
collagen stimulation continues developing gradually over the
following weeks and months as new collagen forms within the skin.
Results vary depending on skin quality, age, lifestyle, and treatment
response, and maintenance treatments are typically recommended yearly
depending on skin ageing and collagen loss.

Possible temporary side effects include redness, tenderness, mild
swelling, and temporary sensitivity.

HIFU is often combined with RF microneedling, skin boosters,
polynucleotides, and medical skincare.
