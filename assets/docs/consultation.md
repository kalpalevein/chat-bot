Every treatment begins with a detailed consultation to understand
your skin concerns, medical history, lifestyle, and goals, allowing a
tailored treatment plan to be created specifically for you. This
includes consultation and assessment, skin analysis, medical history
review, a personalised treatment plan, and treatment and aftercare
guidance. Consultations are suitable for new clients, skin concerns,
acne, pigmentation, ageing concerns, laser treatments, weight
management, and general treatment advice.

Every treatment journey begins with understanding your concerns,
goals and skin health. From consultation through to aftercare,
personalised plans are created around achieving safe, natural and
long-term results. A typical journey follows five stages:
consultation and assessment, personalised treatment plan, treatment,
aftercare support, and review and maintenance.

Initial consultations typically last approximately 30 minutes, though
longer appointments can be arranged for clients with multiple
concerns or more in-depth skincare and treatment planning needs.
Treatment can often be carried out on the same day where suitable and
where appointment availability allows, or alternatively booked for a
later date to fit your schedule and allow time to consider your
options. Consultation fees are fully redeemable against eligible
treatments or medical-grade skincare purchases.

Clients do not need to bring anything specific to a consultation,
though it can help to bring details of any medications, previous
treatment history, medical information, or skincare products
currently in use, as this supports the most appropriate treatment
advice. Clients are encouraged to discuss all of their concerns
during their appointment, and if a more detailed consultation covering
multiple treatment areas is needed, this can be flagged at the time of
booking so sufficient time is allocated.
