Laser hair removal is an advanced treatment designed to reduce
unwanted hair using concentrated light energy. At Skin & Aesthetics
by Haleema, the Derma Diode and Derma Quad Laser systems from Derma
Group are used. The Derma Diode is an advanced triple-wavelength
laser system designed to effectively target fine, medium, and coarse
hair across multiple skin types, combining a 755nm wavelength for
finer hair, an 808nm wavelength for medium and coarse hair, and a
1064nm wavelength for deeper follicles and darker skin tones, along
with advanced cooling technology to improve comfort during treatment.
The Derma Quad Laser combines four wavelengths within one advanced
platform, 755nm Alexandrite, 808nm Diode, 940nm Near Infrared, and
1064nm YAG, allowing treatment to be tailored more effectively across
a wider range of skin tones, hair types, and treatment areas. The
system is designed to improve treatment precision, target hair
follicles at multiple depths, increase comfort, support treatment of
finer and more stubborn hair, and enhance treatment efficiency across
different skin tones.

The laser targets pigment within the hair follicle, damaging the
follicle and reducing future hair growth. Different wavelengths
penetrate at varying depths to target different hair types and skin
tones more effectively.

This treatment is suitable for clients wanting long-term hair
reduction. Suitability depends on skin tone, hair colour, medical
history, hormonal influences, and hair thickness and density.
Treatment may not be suitable for anyone who is pregnant, has active
skin infections or irritation, has recently used fake tan, has
recently waxed or plucked the treatment area, or has certain
photosensitive medical conditions or medications.

Before treatment, clients should avoid waxing or plucking, shave the
area beforehand, avoid fake tan and sun exposure, attend a patch
test, and inform their practitioner of medications or medical
conditions. During treatment, the laser device delivers pulses of
light into the treatment area, with cooling technology used
throughout to maximise comfort and protect the skin. Treatment time
varies depending on the treatment area.

Mild redness and sensitivity may occur temporarily after treatment.
Hair shedding usually occurs over the following weeks as treated
follicles are gradually expelled, and over a course of treatments,
hair growth typically becomes thinner, lighter, and patchier.

Possible temporary side effects include redness, swelling,
sensitivity, and temporary pigment changes.

A course of sessions, usually 6-10, is required for optimal
long-term hair reduction, with maintenance sessions recommended 1-2
times yearly. Patch testing helps assess skin response and treatment
suitability. Suitability for darker skin tones depends on the device
used, skin tone, hair type, and consultation assessment, and multiple
wavelengths are beneficial because different wavelengths target
different hair depths, textures, and skin tones, allowing a more
customised treatment approach.

Laser hair removal is often combined with pigmentation treatments,
medical skincare, and hydrafacial.
